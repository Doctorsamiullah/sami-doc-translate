# 26. PRIVACY-POLICY.md
## Privacy Policy (Draft - Pending Legal Review)

**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026

---

### 1. Fundamental Privacy Guarantee
DocTranslate ("the Software") is designed to safeguard your confidentiality. The software performs document parsing, format conversion, and English ↔ Hindi translations **entirely on your local device**. 

### 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Document Contents**: Your documents, text, images, and formatting never leave your computer or mobile device.
- **Personal Identifiable Information (PII)**: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device identifiers.
- **Analytics & Telemetry**: We do not track keystrokes, translation frequency, or document types.
- **Remote Model Logging**: Offline translation models run locally via CTranslate2 and Google ML Kit on-device binaries. No text is transmitted to Google or any third party during offline inference.

### 3. Local Storage on Your Device
The software stores the following items strictly on your local disk:
- **Translation Memory**: A local database caching translated sentence pairs to speed up subsequent tasks.
- **Custom Glossaries**: Terms and custom definitions you create.
- **Checkpoints & Logs**: Temporary operational logs to allow resumption of interrupted multi-page translation jobs.

You have full control over these local files. You may purge or delete them at any time through the in-app Settings.

### 4. Optional Third-Party Online APIs
If you manually configure an optional custom cloud API (e.g. OpenAI or custom endpoint), your document text will be transmitted to that specific third-party provider in accordance with their respective privacy policies. DocTranslate encrypts your API key locally using Windows DPAPI or Android Keystore.
